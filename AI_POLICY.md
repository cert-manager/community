# AI Usage Policy

The cert-manager project has some key rules for AI usage.

We don't discourage AI usage but we do require that people use AI tools responsibly.

- **All AI usage in any form must be disclosed.**
  You should state the tool you used (e.g. Claude Code, Cursor, ...) along with
  the extent that the work was AI-assisted.

- **The human-in-the-loop must fully understand all code.**
  If you can't explain what your changes do and how they interact with the
  greater system without the aid of AI tools, you're unlikely to be
  able to contribute to cert-manager.

- **Interactions can use AI assistance but must have a full human-in-the-loop.**
  This means that any content generated with AI must have been reviewed
  by a human before submission. AI is very good at being overly verbose
  and including noise that distracts from the main point.
  Humans should do their research and trim this down.

- **It's still your responsibility to ensure you have the right to contribute.**
  The cert-manager project requires a DCO signoff on commits, and AI does not change
  anything about that requirement or the meaning of a DCO signoff.

## cert-manager is maintained by humans

Community contributions have been a huge boon to cert-manager and we've all benefited
together from the work we've shared with each other. AI can and does help.

That said: the cert-manager community - whether in issues, PRs or conversations - should be
humans interacting with each other.

Humans are ultimately who will own the responsibility of reviewing and merging code or
responding to conversation, even if those humans use AI to help.

It is rude and disrespectful to approach the community with low-effort, unqualified
work, since it puts the burden of validation on the maintainer. Please be respectful
of everyone's time.
